Questions

Before you sign up

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The playbook uses WordPress, and my contractors handle the building. You learn to sell, manage clients and place jobs.

Do I need agency experience?

No. The course starts from zero: what an agency sells, how to price it, and how to land your first three clients.

How do the contractors work?

You post a job from a fixed menu of services and pay the listed price. A contractor claims it, delivers it, and you approve it. Contractors are independent and subject to availability.

Can I do this part-time?

Yes. Many people start on the side. Because the team does the building, the work is mostly selling and client communication.

Will I make money?

I can't promise that. The examples on this page show how the pricing works. Your results depend on your clients and your effort.
